FEATURE ENGINEERING (13 Features)
================================================================================

1.  frost_days_bloom           | Freeze risk during Jan-Mar bloom period
2.  tmax_mean_grow             | Average max temp, May-Aug growing season
3.  tmin_mean_grow             | Average min temp, May-Aug growing season
4.  precip_total_grow          | Total precipitation May-Aug
5.  precip_rolling_30          | Max 30-day precipitation window
6.  precip_rolling_60          | Max 60-day precipitation window
7.  precip_rolling_90          | Max 90-day precipitation window
8.  temp_variance_grow         | Thermal stability (std dev of max temps)
9.  gdd_total_grow             | Growing degree days (base 50°F)
10. precip_deviation           | Deviation from 30-year mean precip
11. yield_lag1                 | Prior year yield (tree health)
12. year_numeric               | Calendar year (trend)
13. log_yield                  | Log-transformed target variable

All features calculated from daily NOAA weather data + historical yields.
